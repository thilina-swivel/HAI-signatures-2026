How to install it

  1. Visit outlook.office365.com in your web browser.
  2. Log in to your email account.
  3. Click the Settings icon (the gear, top right).
  4. Go to Account → Signatures.
  5. Select + New signature and name it humaniseAI.
  6. Come back to this page, press Copy signature, then click inside the Outlook editing box and paste with Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on Mac).
  7. Under Select default signatures, choose it for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  8. Click Save, then open a new email to check it.
